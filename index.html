Get Started

Up and running in minutes

1

Get Spotify API credentials

Create a free app on the Spotify Developer Dashboard and add http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback as a Redirect URI.

2

Download & run SpotiFollow

Download the latest release, extract it, and double-click run.bat. No installer needed.

3

Connect your account

Enter your Client ID and Secret, click Connect, and authorize in the browser window that opens.

4

Follow your artists

Pick your playlists, browse the artists, select the ones you want, and click Follow Selected.
